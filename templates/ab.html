About Us

Who We Are

We are a small, independent team of developers, designers, and AI enthusiasts passionate about exploring the intersection of technology and human perception. PSL-Scale was born out of curiosity—to see how artificial intelligence can interpret facial features and generate aesthetic insights in a fun, accessible way.


Our Mission

Our goal is to create tools that are engaging, transparent, and respectful of user privacy. PSL-Scale is not about judgment—it's about experimentation, self-awareness, and understanding how machines "see" us. We believe in empowering users with knowledge, not labels.


Why We Built PSL-Scale

In a world increasingly shaped by algorithms, we wanted to demystify how AI evaluates visual data. PSL-Scale offers a glimpse into that process—without storing your images, without profiling you, and without pretending to be absolute. It's a tool for exploration, not evaluation.


Our Values

  • Privacy First: We never store or share your uploaded images. They are deleted automatically every hour.
  • Transparency: We clearly explain how the AI works and what its limitations are.
  • Respect: We do not support discrimination, objectification, or misuse of AI-generated results.
  • Curiosity: We encourage users to explore, question, and learn—not to judge or compare.
