Privacy Policy

1. No Image Share or Reuse

At PSL Scale, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we handle your data. Our platform utilizes artificial intelligence (AI) to perform facial analysis based on image comparisons. This process is designed to estimate certain characteristics by scanning facial features using machine learning algorithms. However, we want to make it explicitly clear how your images are treated and the limitations of this technology. We do not use or share any of your uploaded images. The uploaded images are being deleted in every hour


2. AI-Based Facial Comparison

The scanning mechanism relies on AI-driven facial comparison techniques. This means the system analyzes facial features and compares them to a reference dataset to generate an estimated result. It is important to understand that this process does not involve biometric identification or recognition of personal identity. The AI does not "know" who you are—it simply performs pattern matching based on facial geometry.


3. Accuracy and Interpretation

While we strive to provide meaningful insights through our AI tools, the results generated by the PSL Scale scanner are inherently approximate and should not be interpreted as definitive or scientifically validated. The technology is experimental and may produce inconsistent or inaccurate outcomes depending on lighting, image quality, facial expression, and other variables. Therefore, we strongly advise users not to take the results too seriously or make any personal, medical, or psychological decisions based on them.


4. Intended Use

The PSL Scale is intended for entertainment and exploratory purposes only. It is not a diagnostic tool, nor should it be used to assess health, personality, or any other sensitive traits. Users are encouraged to approach the results with curiosity rather than certainty.
