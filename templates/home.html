How to use it?

To get started, simply upload a clear, front-facing photo of your face. The AI system will detect your facial features and compare them against 15 baseline reference faces.

Based on how closely your face resembles one or more of these profiles, the algorithm estimates your position on the PSL scale — a visual similarity scale used for playful analysis.

Keep in mind: the system is not perfect. Lighting, image quality, and facial expressions can affect the result. It may occasionally misinterpret features or make inaccurate placements.

This tool is meant for fun and curiosity — not for serious evaluation or judgment. Please treat the output as a light-hearted estimation, not a definitive statement.

How does it work?

The system uses facial landmark detection and similarity scoring algorithms to analyze your photo. It identifies key features like eye spacing, jawline shape, and skin texture, then compares them to a curated set of reference faces. These comparisons generate a percentile score and descriptive feedback.

Privacy & Data

Your uploaded image is processed locally, never shared and is deleted within an hour. We do not perform identity recognition, and no personal data is retained. The analysis is anonymous and temporary, designed solely for entertainment purposes.
